## Pre-flight, for the record

chr1, 17.2 s: 803 isolated genes, 662,995 sense reads, **49.0% 5'-truncated** (median per gene
63.2%), rising 3.10x with transcript length — the RT-processivity shape the survival model assumes.
Verdict: substantial, `--assign em` warranted. Caveat: restricted to the 571 genes with an observed
TSS peak (87% of reads) it is 41.5%; the 232 genes falling back to the annotated TSS sit at 98.5%
and inflate the headline.

## Caveats — read these before trusting any number above

1. **Half the contested reads cannot be checked.** 47% carry no junctions, so the audit is blind to
   them. Today's leak fixes cut the antisense rate there from 17.2% to 9.7%, but that is an
   improvement in a *suspicious signal*, not a measured accuracy gain.
2. **The TE-in-TE case is untested on real data.** Pure TE-vs-TE contests are 0.9% of contested
   reads on chr1, 0.3% on chr2, and **zero** are junction-validatable — TEs have no annotated
   introns. Every validated read involves a gene. The nested-LTR biology is simulation-evidence only.
3. **Antisense is still the largest remaining error class**: 849 wrong calls on chr1 after the fix,
   38% of remaining errors, with a median posterior of 1.000. Confidently wrong.
4. **`EM_Reads` has no uncertainty.** Point estimates go to DESeq2 as though observed. See
   `BOOTSTRAP_DESIGN.md`.
5. **Annotation quality propagates silently.** 32–37% of gene sense units have no observed peak in
   their promoter zone (`Promoter_Anchor = annotated`); 62 chr1 genes have the observed peak more
   than 500 bp from the annotation.
6. **Everything is one library, one genotype, ONT cDNA, two chromosomes.** Every threshold and
   default was set on that.
7. **A README heuristic was wrong and has been softened.** "Distrust units with `Unique_Reads` ≤ 1"
   fails on chr2, where a gene with `Unique_Reads` = 1 legitimately holds 197,685 reads because its
   overlap partner makes almost every read contested. Low `Unique_Reads` means "everything here is
   contested", not "phantom". The real phantom signature was low `Unique_Reads` **and** a tiny span
   **and** implausible splicing.
8. **Simulation percentages changed denominator.** Scenarios G and H were added today, taking clean
   contested reads 230 → 408 and hard 171 → 372. Clean EM now reads 321/408 (78.7%) and hard
   261/372 (70.2%). Scenarios A–F are individually unchanged. **Scenario H is deliberately failing
   at 0/87** — it is the unspliced ground-truth case, left open on purpose.

## Deliberately not done

- **The unspliced-read signal** (`Readout_Frac` versus feature length). Not implemented, by request:
  it would put genuine read-out at risk, and read-out retention was the priority. Scenario H exists
  as its ground-truth test when someone does build it.
- **Posterior recalibration.** Measured at +0.07 points at best, −0.38 at a 0.99 threshold. Not
  worth a flag.
- **`init_by_read` collision across BAMs.** Keyed `(read_name, ref_start, ref_end)` with no sample
  component, so identical query names at identical coordinates in two BAMs share a `TSS_Init_Prob`.
  Fixing it means adding a column to the per-read tables and breaking the regression check. Edge
  case, left in place knowingly.

## Guard-rails that earned their keep

Worth knowing before changing anything in `nested_em.py`:

- **Simulation scenario C** is a TE read-out transcript that initiates in the TE and splices with
  the *gene's* junctions. It caught two over-corrections today, both dropping it 37/37 → 26/37.
  Junction identity must never override initiation evidence.
- **Today's second catch** was subtler: gating initiation on the model's posterior left `GI` at zero
  on the first iteration, so nothing qualified as initiated and read-out failed before the EM had
  seen anything. `GI` is now warm-started from geometry.
- **Run the harness with the pre-patch script as its argument**, not bare:
  `bash test/nested_em/run_nested_em_test.sh <pre-patch IsoClassifier.py>`. Run bare it compares
  against `bfa5a84`, which lacks the unrelated `--tss-window-unnested` work, and falsely reports
  four regressions.

## Recommended next steps

### 1. Close the validation gap with PacBio and CAGE — highest value

The 47% of contested reads that carry no junctions cannot be scored by `junction_audit.py`, and
that is where the remaining antisense calls concentrate (9.7% versus 1.9%). Both data types needed
to fix this already exist on disk:

- **PacBio**: `/data/caleb/PaperWritingReruns/LTRRT_PromSuite/Dec2025_BugFix/AccuMap/PB/PB_AllTissuesMerge_MapQ30.sorted.bam`
  (plus the IsoSeq FLNC sources under `Revisions/Raw_FASTQ/B73_isoseq_flnc/`). FLNC reads are
  full-length-enriched, so their 5' ends are near-true TSSs — exactly what ONT lacks, and it works
  on unspliced reads.
- **CAGE**: `/data/caleb/TSSwork/CAGE_TSS` and `GenBio_25/CAGE`, with `scripts/standardize_cage.sh`
  already in the repo.

Either gives independent per-locus TSS evidence. The audit it enables is different in kind from the
junction audit: it scores a **unit's initiation claim** rather than a **read's origin**. A unit with
substantial `EM_TSS_Reads` and no PacBio 5' ends or CAGE tags in its promoter zone is the phantom
signature, and that test applies to every unit regardless of splicing. Not yet drafted.

### 2. Inferential replicates for `EM_Reads`

See `test/nested_em/BOOTSTRAP_DESIGN.md` — design only, nothing implemented. It covers what a
replicate is, the explicit warning that bootstrapping measures precision and not accuracy (today's
confidently-wrong antisense calls would bootstrap tight), where the cost actually goes, and a
minimal first pass: an `--em-bootstrap N` flag wrapping the existing `resolve()` call in process.
Two open questions are flagged there and probably want answering first — whether annotation
uncertainty belongs in the resample, and whether the downstream path is swish rather than DESeq2.

### 3. Smaller

- Validate on a second library and genotype before anything is published.
- Re-derive gene TSSs from CAGE if the data supports it; it sits upstream of every promoter zone.
- Performance only if iterating heavily or adopting bootstrapping — the two are coupled. Today's
  antisense prior already cut the chr1 EM stage from 313 s to 134 s.
